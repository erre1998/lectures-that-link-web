                    Method and data quality

                    Each affiliation city is compared with the host locations recorded for its lecture series. Local (same city) takes precedence over national (same country); otherwise international connections are classified by the affiliation country's continent. Each type is counted at most once per lecture record. Missing or unresolved geography may prevent classification.

                        Shares use all lecture records in the selected series or corpus. Topics and areas may overlap. Searching topic labels only filters the individual topic table; it does not change the ranking chart or denominator.

                        Method and annotation coverage

                        Existing LLM-generated keywords are normalized for case and whitespace and counted once per lecture record. The four topic areas use word-boundary matching of selected keywords; they are not an exhaustive taxonomy. No new annotations are generated.
