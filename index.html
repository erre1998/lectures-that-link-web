          Method and data quality

          Each affiliation city is compared with the host locations recorded for its lecture series. Local (same city) takes precedence over national (same country); otherwise international connections are classified by the affiliation country's continent. Each type is counted at most once per lecture record. Missing or unresolved geography may prevent classification.
